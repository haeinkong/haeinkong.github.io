## About Me

Hello! 👋 I'm Haein, a PhD candidate in Information Science at Rutgers University (Advisor: Prof. Vivek K. Singh).

My research examines persuasion and manipulation in human and AI-generated language, with a focus on their implications for AI safety and LLM evaluation.

I'm on the 2026–2027 job market, seeking industry Research Scientist positions in AI safety, LLM evaluation, and related areas.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect or collaborate!

## Research Interests

- **AI Safety & LLM Evaluation** 
- **Responsible AI, Fairness & Bias** 
- **Persuasion, Manipulation, & Psycholinguistics**
